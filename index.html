Crystal Miner Simulator

A free-to-play mining simulator on Roblox. Mine glowing crystals, sell them for coins, upgrade your pickaxe, hatch element pets and defeat a boss in every cave on your way to the Void Titan. New updates every other Friday.

⛏️ Play on Roblox Follow us

What you can do

  • 💎

    Mine five crystal caves

    Starter Mine, Amethyst Cavern, Gold Vault, Meteor Crater and Void Rift. Golden crystals pay 10x and meteor showers drop 15x meteor crystals.

  • ⚔️

    Beat the cave bosses

    Each cave is guarded by a boss. Win to open the next cave, then team up to face the Void Titan at the end of the Void Rift.

  • 🐾

    Hatch 20 element pets

    Common to Mythic, with odds always shown. Fuse pets into Golden and Rainbow versions and fill the Pet Index for up to +60% coins.

  • ⛏️

    Upgrade and rebirth

    8 pickaxes, 7 backpacks, rebirths (you keep your pets), a 37-quest chain, 7-day daily rewards and live leaderboards.

The legend of the Heartstone

In the land of Lumenreach, a crystal as big as a mountain once lit the whole world from below. When a greedy baron's drill shattered it, the light scattered across five caves. You're the miner who can hear crystals sing. Free the five Guardians and bring the light back.
