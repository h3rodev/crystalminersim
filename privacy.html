Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by Crystal Miner Simulator, an independent game developer ("we", "us"), through:

  • this website, h3rodev.github.io/crystalminersim (the "Website");
  • our game Crystal Miner Simulator on Roblox (the "Game");
  • our official social media accounts (@crystalminersim on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram); and
  • the tools we use to publish our own posts to those accounts (the "Publishing Tools").

Short version: the Website collects nothing. The Game saves your gameplay progress against your Roblox user ID so you keep it between sessions. We never ask for your real name, address, phone number or payment details, and we never sell data.

1. The Website

The Website is a set of static pages. It has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no forms, no accounts and no third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from the same site, so your browser doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other third party when you visit.

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and secure the service. We do not receive or have access to those logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to reply to you.

2. The Game on Roblox

The Game runs entirely on the Roblox platform. Your Roblox account, chat, friends, payments and parental controls are handled by Roblox Corporation under the Roblox Privacy and Cookie Policy. We don't receive your Roblox password, email address, phone number, real name, date of birth or payment details.

What the Game stores

To save your progress, the Game uses Roblox's built-in storage (Roblox DataStores), keyed to your numeric Roblox user ID:

DataWhy
Gameplay progress: coins, crystals, pickaxe and backpack levels, unlocked caves and defeated bosses, rebirths, pets and Pet Index, quest progress, daily-reward streak, active boosts, redeemed codes, seasonal event progressSo your progress is still there when you come back.
Gameplay statistics (for example crystals mined, eggs opened, monsters and bosses defeated)Quests, achievements and leaderboards.
Purchase receipt IDs for in-game items bought with RobuxSo each purchase is delivered exactly once. Roblox processes the purchase; we only see the item and a receipt ID, never payment details.
Leaderboard scores (user ID + score)Shown on the in-game leaderboards together with your public Roblox username, which the Game looks up from Roblox.

Other Roblox features the Game uses

  • Roblox Analytics. The Game sends gameplay events through Roblox's AnalyticsService (for example when a boss fight starts, is won or lost, or how event currency is earned and spent). We see these only as statistics in the Roblox Creator Dashboard and use them to balance and improve the Game.
  • Purchases. Game passes and in-game items are sold through Roblox. The Game checks with Roblox whether you own a game pass. Roblox's policy service tells the Game whether certain paid items are allowed in your region or for your account, so it can hide them.
  • Moderation. Our staff can remove players who break the rules, using Roblox's kick and ban tools. Staff role assignments are stored by Roblox user ID, and a short log of staff actions is kept in the server's memory only while that server is running.
  • Chat. In-game chat is Roblox's text chat, filtered and moderated by Roblox. We don't store chat messages.

The Game does not use any outside servers, advertising networks or tracking services of its own.

3. Our social media accounts

When you view, follow, like, comment on or message our accounts on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, that information is processed by the platform under its own privacy policy: TikTok, Google/YouTube, Instagram (Meta). We can see what the platform shows any account owner, such as public comments and aggregated statistics in each platform's analytics. We use this only to run our accounts and reply to people.

4. Social media publishing tools

We run a scheduling tool on a server we control to publish our own videos and posts (game clips, update announcements) to our own accounts. To do this, we connect our developer app to platforms such as TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API), YouTube (YouTube API Services) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram APIs).

What we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (for example account ID, display name and avatar), so the tool can show which account it is posting to.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos and posts to that account, and to read the status of those posts.
  • The access tokens the platform issues when the account owner signs in and approves the connection.

How we use it

  • Only to publish content we created to the account that authorised it, and to check that publishing worked.
  • Access tokens are stored on our own server, are not shared with anyone, and are used only to make the requests above.
  • We do not read private messages, collect followers' personal data, build profiles, show ads or sell or rent any data.
  • The tool is for our own accounts. If anyone else ever connects an account, the same rules apply to their data.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.

How to revoke access

  • TikTok: Profile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions (or "Manage app permissions"), then remove the app.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions, then remove access.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings, Apps and websites (or Instagram Settings, Apps and websites), then remove the app.

When access is revoked, the stored token stops working. You can also ask us to delete any stored token and profile data, see section 7.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is processed only by the platforms named above (Roblox, GitHub, TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta) as part of their own services, or if we are legally required to disclose it.

6. Retention and security

  • Game progress is kept while the Game is live so you can continue playing, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Publishing-tool tokens are kept only while the connection is active and deleted when an account is disconnected.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your message.

We use the platforms' own security, keep our server and accounts protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and limit access to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to a minimum.

7. Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to access or delete data linked to you:

  • Game data: email us with your Roblox username, or make a request through Roblox. When Roblox sends us a "right to erasure" request, we delete the stored data for that Roblox user ID.
  • Publishing tool data: revoke access as described above and email us with the subject "Data deletion". We delete any stored tokens and profile data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, or California) you may have additional rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

8. Children

The Game is published on Roblox and follows Roblox's community standards, age guidelines and parental controls. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the Game stores only gameplay data tied to a Roblox user ID, and never asks for a name, email, age or contact details. Our social media accounts are intended for users who meet each platform's minimum age (13 or older). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced on our social accounts.
